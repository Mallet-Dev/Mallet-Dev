# Taza Alsammak

I'm the office manager at Mallet Tile Installation, a tile contractor in Ontario. I started writing software to fix problems in my own job: estimates took too long, call notes got lost, and AI coding agents forgot what they had done. Most of what I build runs our business every day.

I'm still learning, and I work with AI coding agents. I keep that honest by putting every project under version control, running automated tests on every change, and writing down what each tool can and cannot do.

## Public projects

- **[codex-lcm](https://github.com/Mallet-Dev/codex-lcm)**: keeps a full, searchable history of OpenAI Codex CLI sessions in SQLite, summarizes older context without deleting it, and serves it back through a local MCP server. Beta, macOS. Python.
- **[omni-resume](https://github.com/Mallet-Builds/omni-resume)**: one search across the session histories of several coding-agent tools (Claude Code, Codex, Hermes and others), with resume where each tool supports it. A fork of fast-resume. Unfinished, work in progress. Python.
- **[open-seo (fork)](https://github.com/Mallet-Dev/open-seo)**: adds local map-rank evidence, saved reports and project-scoped research to an open-source SEO workbench. TypeScript, Docker.

## Private work (for Mallet Tile)

- An estimating app and backend that price tile jobs from measured plans (TypeScript).
- A searchable archive of business calls, used to find what a customer asked for (Python).
- The company website and an architectural visualization site.
- The agent workspace that coordinates all of this, with shared rules, routing and CI checks.

## How I work

- Every product lives in its own repository with automated checks on every change.
- Small, reviewable changes on short-lived branches.
- Plain-language READMEs that say what works and what does not.

## Stack
